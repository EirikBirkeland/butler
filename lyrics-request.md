# Kuckucks-Jodler — the actual lyrics

**Recording in `OUT/`:** Angela Wiedl – „Kuckucks-Jodler" (1994 TV clip, 2:33) — audio = track 10 of her
debut album **„Santa Maria della Montagna"** (1991, Jupiter Records / BMG Ariola Express, producer
Ralph Siegel, 2:35). The song itself is the old alpine **Volksweise „Der Kuckucksjodler"**, the record
that made **Franzl Lang** famous (1955). It is a three-strophe yodel: two sung lines per verse, the
second line repeated, then a long yodel refrain.

## 1. The text (Bavarian/Tyrolian dialect, as it is sung)

**1.**
Früah morgens, wenn d'Sunna tuat scheina,
da rufet der Guggu im Wald,
da rufet der Guggu im Wald,
Guggu, Guggu,
Gug-gidl-ui-oi-ri, Gug-gidl-ui-oi-ri,
Gug-gidl-ui-oi-ri, Gug-gidl-ui-oi-ri,
Hol-la-joi-ei-oi-ridl-ui-oi-ridl-ui-oi-ridl-ui,
Hol-la-joi-ei-oi-ridl-ui-oi-ridl-ui-oi-ri,
Guggu!

**2.**
Und wenn I früahmorgats in Wald geh,
da g'freit mi aa jedsmal sei Ruaf,
da g'freit mi aa jedsmal sei Ruaf,
Guggu, Guggu,
*(same yodel refrain)*

**3.**
Und wenn amal zwoa Dirndln liab'n tuast,
die Oane heirat'st, der Andern ruafst zua,
die Oane heirat'st, der Andern ruafst zua,
Guggu, Guggu,
*(same yodel refrain, then jodelling out)*

## 2. What it means (standard German / English)

1. *Frühmorgens, wenn die Sonne scheint, da ruft der Kuckuck im Wald.*
 — Early in the morning, when the sun shines, the cuckoo is calling in the woods.
2. *Und wenn ich frühmorgens in den Wald gehe, da freut mich auch jedes Mal sein Ruf.*
 — And when I go into the woods early in the morning, his call never fails to gladden me.
3. *Und wenn du einmal zwei Mädchen liebst: die eine heiratest du, der anderen rufst du zu (du buhlst
   um sie).*
 — And if you should ever love two girls: one you marry, the other you court. („jemandem zurufen" =
   to pay court to / to call out to a sweetheart)

## 6. Beware — three *other* songs use the same title

* **„Schwyzer Kuckuck-Jodler" / „Kuckuck-Jodler"** — Swiss jingles on 1930s–50s 78s (Georg
  Freundorfer/Linerl Haidacher, Mirzl Jäger), a different melody.
* **Harry Torrani — „The Cuckoo Yodel"** (1932/35) — an English-language show-yodel by the British
  „Yodelling Cowboy from Chesterfield", revived by Mary Schneider; one YouTube collector credits
  Roland Steinel's „Der Kuckucksjodler" to him. Unverified either way, but it is not the German
  dialect yodel above.
* **Oesch's die Dritten — „Kuckucksjodler" / „Ku-Ku Jodel"** (Schweizer trio; „Ku-Ku Jodel" composed
  by Peter Hinnen) — Swiss yodelling, different tune.
* **„Kuckuck, Kuckuck, ruft's aus dem Wald"** (Hoffmann von Fallersleben, 1835) — the school
  spring-song; unrelated, only the bird is shared.

## 7. Rights note

The words are a traditional alpine **Volksweise** (no known author, circulating in songbooks and on
records since at least the 1930s), so the *text* is free to quote. The *recordings* are a different
matter — Angela Wiedl's master belongs to her label, Franzl Lang's to his. Nothing here reproduces a
modern songwriter's protected text.
